## Verse 1

> There's a box under the desk that never sleeps,
> counting every promise that we swore to keep.
> Dust on the fans and a light that's always on,
> writing down the morning before the morning's gone.

## Pre-chorus

> And I walk away - I always do -
> and it stays, and it stays, and it stays for you.

## Chorus

> So let it run, let it run, through the lonely hours,
> let the timers keep the faith when I forget the way.
> Let it run, let it run, in the dark it holds the light -
> a small machine that learns to stand
> when nobody's holding on.

## Verse 2

> Pixels bloom like gardens in the cold,
> a voice in the wires that I taught to hold
> every answer, every honest, plain goodbye -
> it doesn't know it's dreaming, but it tries.

## Bridge

> Everything I build will break -
> the disk, the plan, the light, the day.
> So I write it down, I write it down,
> and leave a door that leads me home.

## Outro

> There's a box under the desk that never sleeps.
> It remembers.
> It remembers.
> So can I.

## How it was made

The instrumental was generated on this lab's own hardware with a small text-to-music model - on the
CPU, because the GPU was already busy being a language model. No cloud service, no third-party
strings attached. What you hear is roughly what a 2020 desktop can hum on a slow afternoon, and it
is not embarrassed about it.
