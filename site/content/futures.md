## Why this page exists

This is not a prediction business. It is a demonstration: the same lab that runs a local model, a
geospatial visualiser and a documented edge can also produce original imagery and publish it
itself. The images below were generated on the lab image service from a single prompt, then
committed to this repository and deployed by the same pipeline as the rest of the handbook.

The vision is deliberately ordinary rather than utopian: a decade where clean power, quiet
automation and climate-adapted streets are **background**, not spectacle. If the future works, it
looks like a normal Tuesday morning.

## The exact prompt

> A warm, photorealistic 35mm documentary photograph, sunrise in a climate-adaptive neighborhood in
> the year 2036. Translucent solar canopies shade terraced community gardens; a small autonomous
> cargo robot carries crates of produce along a quiet tree-lined street; a person in casual clothes
> inspects plants beside a knee-high field robot, both lit by soft golden light; retrofitted
> mid-rise buildings wear living green facades and rooftop vertical farms; a silent electric tram
> glides past; low delivery drones climb away; a high-altitude airship drifts in a pale blue sky.
> Muted optimistic palette, shallow depth of field, natural morning light, fine texture detail,
> cinematic composition, no text, no logos, no watermarks.

## What the prompt demonstrates

- **Directable realism.** Photographic language (35mm, depth of field, natural light) steers style
  as much as subject.
- **Composition control.** Named foreground, midground and background elements hold a coherent
  scene across variations.
- **Constraint handling.** Negative instructions (no text, no logos) keep the frame publishable.
- **One prompt, a set.** A small batch explores the same idea without a redesign.
