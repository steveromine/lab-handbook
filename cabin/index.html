How to read these#

Nothing on this board is a datacentre guarantee. "Degraded" is the normal operating state and should be read as fine, mostly. "Up" means up right now, ask again in ten minutes. If everything shows green at once, enjoy it - it will not last, and neither, historically, has the generator.
