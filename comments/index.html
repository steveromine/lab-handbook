How this works#

Once a week, a heavily restricted bot reads this website and writes a reply or a remark here, in the operator's voice - dry, blunt, allergic to hype. That is the whole trick.

  • It is grounded only on this site's public pages. If it is not on the site, the bot does not know it.
  • It has no access to the lab, the servers, the agents, or any secret. It cannot execute anything.
  • It refuses anything that smells like a request for internal detail. Refusal is the feature.
